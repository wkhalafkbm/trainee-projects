# Brand Warranty and Service Policies

**Document:** Group Customer Care — Warranty Terms by Brand
**Applies To:** Contact Centre Agents, Digital Customer Care Assistant, Brand Service Teams
**Version:** 3.1
**Effective Date:** January 2025
**Issuing Department:** Group Customer Experience Office

---

## Purpose

This document sets out the warranty and service terms for each brand under the Group. Terms are deliberately similar in structure across brands (all use a manufacturer warranty plus an optional extended warranty), but the specific durations, coverage, and exclusions differ by brand. Do not assume one brand's terms apply to another — always confirm the brand first using `brand-directory-and-scope.md`.

---

## Automotive Division

### Toyota (Group Motors)

- **Manufacturer warranty:** 5 years or 150,000 km, whichever comes first, covering manufacturing defects in engine, transmission, and electrical systems
- **Corrosion warranty:** 8 years, unlimited mileage, covering perforation from internal corrosion only
- **Extended warranty (optional, purchased separately):** Available up to 7 years or 210,000 km from original purchase date; must be purchased within 90 days of the manufacturer warranty's start date
- **Exclusions:** Wear items (brake pads, tyres, wiper blades), damage from off-road use outside manufacturer specifications, damage from unauthorised modifications
- **Service intervals:** Every 10,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first — missed scheduled services may void the manufacturer warranty for the affected component

### Lexus (Group Motors)

- **Manufacturer warranty:** 6 years or 200,000 km, whichever comes first, covering manufacturing defects in engine, transmission, and electrical systems
- **Corrosion warranty:** 10 years, unlimited mileage, covering perforation from internal corrosion only
- **Extended warranty (optional, purchased separately):** Available up to 8 years or 240,000 km from original purchase date; must be purchased within 60 days of the manufacturer warranty's start date
- **Exclusions:** Wear items, damage from off-road use, damage from unauthorised modifications, damage from use of non-approved parts during servicing
- **Service intervals:** Every 10,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first

**Note the difference from Toyota:** longer warranty duration, shorter extended-warranty purchase window, and an additional exclusion (non-approved parts) not present in the Toyota terms. A customer service response that applies Toyota's terms to a Lexus vehicle (or vice versa) will misstate the coverage in both directions.

### FleetCare Commercial

- Fleet vehicles are covered under individually negotiated commercial contracts, not the standard consumer warranty terms above
- The digital assistant must not quote a warranty duration for any fleet vehicle enquiry — direct all fleet warranty questions to the Commercial Accounts Team, as terms vary by contract

## Electronics Division

### Yiaco Electronics (Home Appliances)

- **Manufacturer warranty:** 2 years parts and labour on major appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioning units); 1 year on small kitchen appliances
- **Extended warranty (optional):** Available up to an additional 3 years on major appliances only, purchased at time of sale or within 30 days
- **Exclusions:** Damage from incorrect installation by a non-authorised technician, damage from voltage fluctuation without a certified surge protector, cosmetic damage
- **Repair process:** Authorised service partner visit within 3–5 business days of a valid claim; if the unit cannot be repaired on-site, collection and return within 10 business days

### Yiaco Mobile (Phones and Tablets)

- **Manufacturer warranty:** 1 year parts and labour, manufacturer-defined (varies by device manufacturer; Yiaco Mobile administers the claim locally)
- **Extended warranty / device protection plan (optional):** Available at time of purchase only — cannot be added after the sale — covering accidental damage and one battery replacement
- **Exclusions:** Water damage (unless the device protection plan is active and includes liquid damage cover), damage from unauthorised repair attempts, lost or stolen devices
- **Repair process:** In-store diagnostic within 24 hours; repair or replacement within 5–7 business days depending on parts availability

**Note the difference from Yiaco Electronics:** shorter warranty duration, device protection plan must be purchased at point of sale (unlike the 30-day window for Electronics' extended warranty), and different exclusions entirely (water damage vs. voltage/installation issues). These are administered by different teams despite the shared "Yiaco" brand name.

## Consumer Retail Division

### HomeStyle Retail (Furniture and Homeware)

- **Manufacturer warranty:** 1 year on manufacturing defects for furniture; 90 days on homeware and decor items
- **Delivery damage claims:** Must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence; claims made after 48 hours are assessed case-by-case and are not guaranteed
- **Returns:** 14 days from delivery for unused items in original packaging; assembled furniture cannot be returned unless defective
- **Exclusions:** Normal wear, damage from improper assembly, fabric or upholstery staining after use

### Group Fashion Outlets

- **Returns:** 30 days from purchase with receipt, unused and with tags attached
- **Exchanges:** Permitted in-store only, subject to stock availability at the specific store location
- **Faulty items:** Full refund or replacement regardless of the 30-day window, if the fault is a manufacturing defect confirmed by store staff
- **Exclusions:** Sale/clearance items are final sale and not eligible for return unless faulty; swimwear and earrings are non-returnable for hygiene reasons regardless of condition

## Commitment Boundary (Applies to All Brands)

The digital assistant may **explain** any of the terms above and **confirm what a customer is eligible for** based on stated facts (purchase date, product type, fault description). The assistant may not:

- Confirm that a specific claim **will** be approved — approval requires inspection or verification by the relevant brand's service team
- Promise a specific refund amount, replacement unit, or compensation
- Waive a stated exclusion or policy term on the customer's behalf

Where a customer's situation appears to qualify for a resolution, the assistant should state what the policy allows and then hand off to the relevant brand's service or escalation team (see `brand-directory-and-scope.md`) to confirm and action it.
