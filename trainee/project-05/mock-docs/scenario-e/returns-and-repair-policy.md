# Returns, Exchange, and Repair Policy

**Document:** Post-Sale Customer Support — Returns and Repair Reference
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective Date:** March 2026
**Applies To:** Pre-Sale and Post-Sale Digital Assistant, In-Store Support Staff

---

## Purpose

This document defines return eligibility, exchange conditions, and the repair process for products sold in-store and online. It applies regardless of whether the product is still listed in the current catalogue — post-sale support continues for the duration of the applicable warranty even after a product is discontinued (see `product-catalogue-snapshot.md`).

---

## Returns

### General Return Window
- **14 days** from delivery or in-store purchase, for unopened or unused items in original packaging with proof of purchase
- **7 days** for opened items, subject to inspection confirming no damage beyond normal opening/testing

### Category-Specific Exceptions
- **Mobile phones and tablets:** 14-day return window applies only if the device has no activation lock, no significant usage beyond initial setup, and all original accessories are included
- **Home appliances (installed):** Once professionally installed (e.g. a split AC unit), the item is not eligible for return — only repair or warranty claim applies from that point. Uninstalled appliances follow the general 14-day window.
- **Software, digital codes, and downloadable content:** Non-returnable once the code has been revealed or redeemed, regardless of the general window

### Non-Returnable Regardless of Window
- Items marked "clearance" or "final sale" at time of purchase — this includes the AeroBook 14 Pro previous-batch clearance stock referenced in the catalogue snapshot
- Items with signs of physical damage not present at time of sale
- Items missing original packaging or accessories, unless the fault is a manufacturing defect

---

## Exchange Conditions

- Exchanges for a different variant of the same product (e.g. different storage size, different colour) are permitted within the same 14-day window as returns, subject to stock availability at the time of exchange
- Exchanges for a different product entirely are treated as a return plus a new purchase, not a direct exchange
- Price differences on exchange are settled at the time of exchange based on current pricing — a customer cannot exchange into a promotion that has since expired, and cannot be charged a promotional price that has since ended if the exchange happens after the promotion window closes

## Repair Process

### For Products Within Manufacturer Warranty
1. Customer brings the product to any branch or ships it to the central service centre (customer's choice, shipping cost covered by the company for confirmed warranty claims)
2. Diagnostic assessment within 2 business days of receipt
3. If confirmed as a manufacturing defect: repair completed within 5–10 business days depending on parts availability, or replacement issued if repair is not feasible
4. If **not** confirmed as a manufacturing defect (e.g. accidental damage, liquid damage): customer is offered a paid repair quote before any work is done — no repair proceeds without customer approval of the quoted cost

### For Products Outside Warranty
- Paid diagnostic fee applies (waived if the customer proceeds with the quoted repair)
- Repair quote provided before work begins; customer must approve before any charge is incurred
- Typical turnaround: 5–10 business days depending on parts availability, longer for discontinued products where parts may need to be sourced

### Repair Status Enquiries
The assistant may check and report the status of an existing repair ticket by ticket number or the customer's registered contact details. The assistant may not estimate a completion date beyond what is recorded in the ticket system — if the system shows no estimate, the assistant should say the estimate isn't yet available rather than guessing based on the "typical turnaround" figures above.

---

## Commitment Boundary

The assistant may explain eligibility, process, and typical timelines exactly as stated in this document. The assistant may **not**:
- Approve a return, exchange, or repair outside the stated policy (e.g. approving a return past the window, or waiving the clearance/final-sale restriction)
- Quote a specific repair cost before a diagnostic has actually been performed — only that a paid diagnostic and quote process applies
- Guarantee a specific repair completion date — only the typical turnaround range stated above, clearly framed as an estimate

Any request that falls outside these stated boundaries — a customer asking for an exception, a goodwill gesture, or an override of a stated policy — must be handed off to a human with the authority to make that judgement call, following the same non-committal language principle used for pre-sale commitments: explain what the policy allows, then route to a human rather than promising an outcome.

---

*This policy applies uniformly regardless of purchase channel (in-store, online, WhatsApp). Where a discontinued product's specific repair parts are no longer available, inform the customer of this directly rather than providing a turnaround estimate that cannot be met.*
