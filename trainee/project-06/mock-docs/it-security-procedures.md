# IT Security Procedures

**Clearance Level:** General  
**Last Updated:** January 2025  
**Owner:** IT Security Team

---

## Password Policy

All employee accounts must comply with the following password requirements:

- **Minimum length:** 12 characters
- **Complexity:** Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character (e.g. `!@#$%^&*`)
- **Rotation frequency:** Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system will prompt you before expiry.
- **Reuse:** You may not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- **Sharing:** Passwords must never be shared with colleagues, IT staff, or anyone else. IT will never ask for your password.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employees. MFA is enforced at login and when accessing any system classified as Internal or above.

---

## VPN Usage Requirements

Remote access to company systems requires an active VPN connection at all times.

- **VPN client:** GlobalProtect (download from the internal IT portal)
- **When to use:** Any time you are working outside a company office, including from home, client sites, or public locations
- **Prohibited:** Connecting to Internal or Confidential systems from a public WiFi network without VPN is a policy violation
- **Split tunnelling:** Disabled by default. All traffic routes through the VPN while connected.

If the VPN connection drops while you are accessing company systems, your session will be automatically suspended. Reconnect and log in again.

---

## Acceptable Use of Personal Devices

Personal devices (phones, tablets, personal laptops) may be used for work purposes under the following conditions:

- The device must have screen lock enabled with a PIN, password, or biometric
- Company data must not be stored locally on personal devices unless using the approved cloud storage tool (OneDrive for Business)
- Personal devices may not be used to access Confidential or Restricted systems
- If a personal device is lost or stolen, IT Security must be notified within 4 hours so that remote wipe of company data can be initiated

Personal devices are subject to inspection if there is reasonable cause to believe company policy has been violated.

---

## How to Report a Security Incident

A security incident includes: suspected account compromise, phishing emails, malware, lost or stolen devices, and accidental data disclosure.

**If you suspect a security incident:**

1. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate it yourself
2. Do not turn off the affected device (it may contain evidence)
3. Call the IT Security hotline immediately: **ext. 4444** (internal) or **+965 2200 4444** (external)
4. Submit a written incident report via the IT portal within 24 hours

All security incidents are taken seriously. Reporting an incident in good faith will never result in disciplinary action, even if you caused it accidentally.

## Data Classification Levels

All company data is classified into one of four levels. Handling requirements depend on the classification.

| Level | Description | Examples | Sharing Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Information approved for external release | Marketing materials, public website content | May be shared freely |
| **Internal** | General business information not for public release | Internal policies, meeting notes, project plans | Share within the company only |
| **Confidential** | Sensitive business or personal information | HR records, financial data, client contracts | Share on a need-to-know basis; requires manager approval to share externally |
| **Restricted** | Highest sensitivity; regulated or legally protected | Salary bands, acquisition plans, legal advice | Access limited to named individuals; any sharing requires executive approval |

When in doubt about how to classify a document, treat it as the higher level and consult your manager or the IT Security team.
