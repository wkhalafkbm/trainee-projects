# Complaint Service Categories and Department Routing Guide

**Document:** Kuwait Municipality — Public Complaints Management System
**Applies To:** Complaint Intake Officers, Customer Service Representatives, Digital Complaint Portal (Automated Routing)
**Version:** 4.2
**Effective Date:** March 2023
**Issuing Department:** Municipal Affairs and Customer Service Directorate

---

## Purpose

This guide defines the official complaint categories accepted by Kuwait Municipality, the department responsible for each category, the required information to be collected from the complainant, and the standard service level agreement (SLA) for initial response and resolution. All complaint intake personnel must apply this guide consistently to ensure complaints are routed correctly on first contact and SLAs are met.

---

## General Intake Requirements (All Categories)

Before categorising a complaint, intake officers must collect the following information for every submission:

- Complainant's full name (mandatory)
- Civil ID number or residency number (mandatory for follow-up; may be withheld for anonymous complaints per system settings)
- Contact number (mobile preferred)
- Email address (if available)
- Complaint description in the complainant's own words
- Date and time of incident (if applicable)
- Location: governorate, area/block, street, building or plot number
- Whether the complainant has submitted a previous complaint about the same issue (if yes, obtain previous complaint reference number)

> Note: Anonymous complaints are accepted for all categories but cannot be followed up. The system will generate a reference number that the complainant may use to check status online without revealing their identity.

## Category 1: Roads and Infrastructure

**Description:** Complaints relating to the physical condition of roads, bridges, pavements, and associated infrastructure in public areas.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Potholes and road surface deterioration
- Damaged or subsided road surface following utility works
- Cracked or uneven pavements creating a tripping hazard
- Broken, missing, or obscured road signs and directional signs
- Damaged road barriers, guard rails, or central reservation structures
- Damaged speed bumps or road markings that create a traffic hazard
- Damage to bridges or underpasses used by the public

**Handling Department:** Roads and Public Works Department (إدارة الطرق والأشغال العامة)
**Sub-unit routing:** For structural issues (bridges, major roads): Infrastructure Engineering Division. For surface and signage issues: Roads Maintenance Division.

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (safety hazard): 4-hour initial assessment; repair or temporary mitigation within 24 hours
- Standard (non-hazardous): Acknowledgement within 1 business day; repair scheduled within 7 business days
- For major structural works: Acknowledgement within 1 business day; scheduled works plan within 14 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Precise location (area, block, street; GPS coordinates if available via the digital portal)
- Description of the defect (size, nature, whether it is worsening)
- Whether the defect has caused or is likely to cause an accident
- Photographs (strongly recommended; upload via portal or send to designated WhatsApp number)
- Time the defect was first noticed

**Site visit required:** Yes — all roads and infrastructure complaints require a site assessment before repair works can be raised. Initial categorisation as urgent/non-urgent is based on the complainant's description pending site verification.

## Category 2: Street Lighting

**Description:** Complaints relating to public street lighting, pedestrian path lighting, and lighting in public spaces maintained by the municipality.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Non-functioning street lights (single light or multiple lights in a row)
- Entire street or area section without lighting
- Flickering or dimming lights indicating electrical fault
- Damaged light poles (collision damage, corrosion)
- Lights that remain on during daylight hours (wastage/fault)
- Missing or damaged light covers creating an electrical exposure hazard

**Out of scope for this category:** Lighting inside buildings, private property lighting, and lighting within Kuwait Electricity and Water Authority (MEWA) installations — redirect to MEWA.

**Handling Department:** Municipal Engineering and Lighting Department (إدارة الهندسة البلدية والإنارة)
**Sub-unit routing:** Electrical Maintenance Unit

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (exposed electrical hazard, broken pole in a traffic area): 4-hour response
- Standard (non-functioning light, dark area): Repair or replacement within 3 business days
- Scheduled maintenance (intermittent fault, efficiency issue): Within 5 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Location (area, block, street)
- Approximate number of affected lights
- Description of the fault (not working, damaged pole, always on, etc.)
- Whether the dark area is creating a safety risk (pedestrian area, children's play area, school zone)
- Photographs if the fault is visible (damaged pole, visible damage)

**Site visit required:** Yes — an electrician must attend before fault can be confirmed and logged for repair.

## Category 3: Waste Management

**Description:** Complaints relating to the collection, disposal, and management of solid waste in public areas.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Missed scheduled waste collection from a residential or commercial area
- Overflowing waste bins in public spaces or on public roads
- Illegal dumping of waste on public or vacant land
- Abandoned construction debris on public land
- Litter accumulation in public areas not being addressed by scheduled cleaning
- Damaged or vandalised public bins that need repair or replacement
- Complaints about frequency of collection (area not served on schedule)

**Out of scope:** Waste collection from private commercial premises (handled by commercial waste contractors); household hazardous waste (contact Kuwait Environment Public Authority).

**Handling Department:** Cleaning and Waste Collection Department (إدارة النظافة وجمع النفايات)
**Sub-unit routing:** Residential Collection Unit (for missed collections); Public Spaces Cleaning Unit (for litter and bins); Illegal Dumping Enforcement Unit (for illegal dumping complaints)

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (waste posing a public health risk, illegal dumping of hazardous material): 4-hour response
- Standard (missed collection, overflowing bins): Collection within 1 business day; follow-up within 2 business days
- Scheduled (bin replacement, route frequency review): Within 5 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Location of the issue (as specific as possible — block, plot, or GPS)
- Type of waste involved (general household, construction, hazardous)
- How long the waste has been accumulating (for illegal dumping complaints)
- Photographs (mandatory for illegal dumping complaints to support enforcement action)
- For missed collections: day and frequency of normal collection service

**Site visit required:** Yes for illegal dumping (enforcement and evidence gathering). Remote resolution possible for missed collections (dispatch of a collection vehicle without physical inspection required).

## Category 4: Public Parks and Open Spaces

**Description:** Complaints relating to the condition, cleanliness, safety, and maintenance of public parks, open green spaces, public squares, and recreational areas maintained by the municipality.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Damaged playground equipment (broken swings, exposed sharp edges — safety risk)
- Broken benches, picnic tables, or public seating
- Damaged or non-functioning public toilets within parks
- Persistent litter or uncollected waste within the park
- Damaged fencing, gates, or enclosures
- Dead or diseased trees posing a falling hazard
- Vandalism damage to park facilities
- Irrigation failures causing grass/plant damage

**Handling Department:** Parks and Public Spaces Department (إدارة الحدائق والمساحات العامة)
**Sub-unit routing:** Maintenance and Repair Unit (physical damage); Landscaping and Horticulture Unit (plants, trees, irrigation)

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (damaged equipment posing immediate injury risk, fallen tree blocking access): 4-hour response; hazard secured within 24 hours
- Standard (damaged furniture, litter, general maintenance): Assessed within 2 business days; repair scheduled within 5 business days
- Scheduled (cosmetic improvements, new planting requests): Within 10 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Name of the park or open space
- Location within the park (near entrance, playground area, etc.)
- Nature of the damage or concern
- Whether there is an immediate safety hazard (broken equipment, fallen tree)
- Photographs (strongly recommended)

**Site visit required:** Yes for all complaints requiring physical repair. Remote acknowledgement and scheduling can be completed without a site visit.

## Category 5: Noise Complaints

**Description:** Complaints relating to excessive, sustained, or unreasonable noise from construction sites, commercial premises, or other activities in public or semi-public areas.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Construction noise outside permitted working hours (standard permitted hours: 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days; no construction noise on Fridays or public holidays)
- Excessive noise from commercial premises (restaurants, event venues, shops with loud sound systems)
- Persistent noise from industrial activity in a mixed-use or residential area
- Loud generators operating outside permitted hours

**Out of scope:** Residential neighbour noise disputes (these are handled by the Ministry of Interior); noise inside private buildings; noise from mosques (refer to Ministry of Awqaf).

**Handling Department:** Municipal Inspection and Compliance Department (إدارة التفتيش والامتثال البلدي)
**Sub-unit routing:** Noise and Environmental Compliance Unit

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (construction noise at night — after 10:00 PM — or noise causing disturbance to a medical facility or school during hours): 4-hour inspection response
- Standard (commercial noise during permitted hours but at excessive levels): Inspection within 2 business days; warning issued if violation confirmed
- Repeat offender (second confirmed complaint within 30 days): Escalated to Enforcement Unit for penalty proceedings

**Information required from complainant:**
- Source of the noise (construction site, restaurant name, premises address)
- Time and days the noise is occurring
- Duration (how long has this been occurring)
- Whether the noise has been formally reported before
- Contact details for a callback (noise complaints are difficult to verify without real-time attendance — complainant may be contacted for a live inspection)

**Site visit required:** Yes — noise complaints must be verified by an inspector in person. Complainants are advised that inspectors may ask them to call again when the noise is actively occurring to enable real-time inspection.

## Category 6: Building and Planning Violations

**Description:** Complaints relating to suspected unauthorised construction, violations of approved building permits, illegal changes of use, or encroachments on public land.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Construction work being carried out without a visible permit
- Construction that appears to exceed approved dimensions or height limits
- Encroachment onto a public road, pavement, or neighbouring property
- Commercial use of a property zoned for residential purposes
- Unregistered or illegal extensions to existing buildings
- Construction waste or materials stored on public land without authorisation

**Handling Department:** Urban Planning and Building Control Department (إدارة التخطيط العمراني ومراقبة البناء)
**Sub-unit routing:** Building Inspections Unit (for active construction violations); Land Use Compliance Unit (for use violations and encroachments)

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (construction activity presenting imminent structural or public safety risk): 4-hour response; stop-work order authority
- Standard (suspected permit violation, encroachment): Site inspection within 3 business days; formal finding issued within 7 business days
- Scheduled (change of use complaint, minor encroachment): Within 5 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Full address or plot number of the alleged violation
- Description of the violation (what is being built, what the concern is)
- Whether construction is currently active
- Photographs (essential for building violations — provides evidence and helps prioritise)
- Whether the complainant is a neighbour, passing member of the public, or has another relationship to the property (for potential conflict of interest assessment)

**Site visit required:** Yes — all building and planning complaints require a physical inspection. No enforcement action can be taken without an inspector's on-site assessment.

## Category 7: Water and Drainage

**Description:** Complaints relating to water accumulation, flooding, drainage failures, and water leakage on public land or public infrastructure. Note: complaints about domestic water supply or MEWA services should be redirected to MEWA, not the municipality.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Flooding of public roads or public spaces due to heavy rain and drainage failure
- Blocked or overflowing municipal drains or storm water channels
- Standing water on public land that is not clearing (potential mosquito breeding hazard)
- Water leakage from municipal water mains on public land
- Subsidence or road damage caused by underground water leakage

**Out of scope:** Water supply or pressure issues inside buildings (MEWA); sewage backflow inside buildings (Kuwait Central System for Sewage or the relevant utility authority).

**Handling Department:** Infrastructure and Drainage Department (إدارة البنية التحتية والصرف الصحي)
**Sub-unit routing:** Storm Drainage Unit (flooding and blocked drains); Utilities Coordination Unit (water main leaks in coordination with MEWA)

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (road flooding causing traffic hazard; water leakage causing road subsidence): 4-hour response; hazard containment within 24 hours
- Standard (standing water, slow-clearing flooding): Site assessment within 1 business day; drainage clearance within 2 business days
- Scheduled (long-term drainage improvement requests): Assessed within 5 business days; capital works subject to annual planning cycle

**Information required from complainant:**
- Precise location (area, block, street; intersection if relevant)
- Nature of the issue (flooding, blocked drain, leakage — describe what is visible)
- How long the water has been present
- Whether the water is affecting road traffic or pedestrian access
- Photographs (highly recommended — aerial or wide-angle shots showing extent of flooding are most useful)

**Site visit required:** Yes. Urgent cases require immediate site attendance by a drainage engineer.

## Category 8: Public Health

**Description:** Complaints relating to conditions that may pose a risk to public health in public areas or commercial premises subject to municipal health inspections.

**Examples of covered complaints:**
- Rat or vermin infestation in a public area or around a commercial premises
- Pest control concern (mosquito breeding sites, cockroach infestations in a restaurant or food premises)
- Unsanitary conditions at a food premises (restaurant, cafeteria, food truck)
- Stray animals causing a public health concern in a populated area
- Dead animals on public land that have not been removed
- Accumulated waste creating a health hazard (connected to Waste Management — dual-route as appropriate)
- Suspected sale of expired or contaminated food products

**Handling Department:** Public Health and Environmental Inspection Department (إدارة الصحة العامة والتفتيش البيئي)
**Sub-unit routing:** Pest Control Unit (vermin and pest complaints); Food Safety Inspection Unit (food premises and product complaints); Environmental Health Unit (general public health)

**Standard SLA:**
- Urgent (vermin infestation near a school, hospital, or food market; suspected food contamination currently on sale): 4-hour response; immediate inspection
- Standard (pest control request, general unsanitary conditions): Inspection within 2 business days
- Scheduled (regular food premises inspection request following a complaint): Within 3 business days

**Information required from complainant:**
- Location (for pest complaints: describe where the infestation is occurring — building perimeter, open land, restaurant area)
- For food premises: name of the establishment, address, type of business
- Nature of the concern (describe what was observed)
- For food product complaints: name of product, where purchased, batch or expiry information if visible
- Photographs (strongly recommended, particularly for food safety complaints as evidence)
- Whether the complainant requires anonymity (food safety complaints especially — complainant may fear retaliation from a business owner)

**Site visit required:** Yes for all public health complaints. Remote resolution is not possible.
